# Data Classification

## Levels
Lumen data has four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer personal data and source code of the robot controller are Restricted.

## Handling Restricted data
Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems, must be encrypted in transit and at rest, and must never be pasted into external AI tools or public chatbots.

## Sharing externally
Confidential data may be shared with partners only under a signed NDA and through the Secure Share service. Restricted data requires approval from the data owner and the CISO.
