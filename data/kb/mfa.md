# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Who must use MFA
MFA is mandatory for every Lumen account, including contractors and service accounts that support interactive login.

## Approved methods
The approved second factors are the Lumen Authenticator app (push with number matching) and FIDO2 hardware security keys. SMS codes are only allowed as a temporary fallback for up to 7 days.

## Lost or new phone
If you lose your phone or get a new one, open a ticket in the Service Portal under "MFA reset". The Service Desk verifies your identity by video call before resetting MFA.

## MFA fatigue
If you receive an MFA push you did not trigger, deny it and report it to the Security Operations Center (SOC) right away. Repeated unexpected pushes usually mean your password is known to an attacker.
