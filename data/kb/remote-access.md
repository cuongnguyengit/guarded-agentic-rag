# Remote Access and VPN

## VPN requirement
Internal systems are reachable from outside the office only through the Lumen VPN client, Lumen Connect. Split tunneling is disabled.

## Public Wi-Fi
On public Wi-Fi, connect to the VPN before opening any work application. Do not use hotel or airport computers for work.

## Travel abroad
Before travelling to another country with a work laptop, file a travel notice in the Service Portal at least 5 business days in advance so access from that region is not blocked.
