# Password Policy

## Requirements
Passwords for Lumen accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of four or more unrelated words are recommended. Do not reuse a password that you use for any personal service.

## Password manager
Every employee must store work credentials in the company password manager, Keyvault Desk. Writing passwords on paper or in plain-text files is not allowed.

## Rotation
Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. You must change your password immediately if you suspect it was exposed, for example after entering it on a phishing page.

## Sharing
Never share your password with anyone, including the IT Service Desk. IT staff will never ask for your password by chat, phone or e-mail.
