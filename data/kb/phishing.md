# Reporting Phishing

## How to report
Use the "Report Phish" button in the mail client. If the button is not available, forward the message as an attachment to phishing@lumen.example.

## What not to do
Do not click links, open attachments or reply to the sender. Do not forward a suspicious e-mail to colleagues to ask whether it is real.

## If you already clicked
If you clicked a link or entered credentials, change your password immediately and call the SOC hotline at extension 5555. Speed matters more than certainty.

## Response time
The SOC triages reported phishing within 30 minutes during business hours and confirms the verdict back to the reporter.
