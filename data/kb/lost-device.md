# Lost or Stolen Devices

## Report immediately
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 1 hour through the SOC hotline at extension 5555. After hours, the hotline is still staffed.

## What happens next
The SOC locks the device remotely, revokes active sessions and, if needed, wipes the device. Disk encryption protects data on company laptops, but sessions and tokens must still be revoked.

## Police report
For stolen devices, also file a police report and attach the reference number to the Service Portal ticket.
