# Access Requests

## Requesting access
Request access to applications through the Service Portal under "Access request". Each request must name a business reason and is approved by your manager and the system owner.

## Privileged access
Administrator rights are granted just-in-time for at most 8 hours through the Privileged Access Manager. Standing admin accounts are not allowed.

## Access reviews
Managers review their team's access every quarter. Access that is not confirmed in the review is removed automatically.
