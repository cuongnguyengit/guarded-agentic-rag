# Security Incident Response

## What counts as an incident
A security incident is any event that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Lumen data or systems: malware, a lost device with company data, unauthorized access, or data sent to the wrong recipient.

## Reporting
Report incidents to the SOC through the Service Portal (category "Security incident") or the SOC hotline at extension 5555, available 24/7.

## Severity levels
Incidents are classified as SEV1 (critical, active compromise), SEV2 (high), SEV3 (medium) or SEV4 (low). SEV1 incidents page the on-call security lead within 15 minutes.

## Escalation to L2
The SOC L1 analyst escalates to L2 when malware is confirmed on more than one machine, when privileged accounts are involved, or when customer data may be affected.
